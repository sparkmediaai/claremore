Questions

Fractional leadership, explained.

What does “fractional” mean?

You get an experienced executive for a portion of their time, instead of a full-time hire. They lead the function, set strategy and oversee execution, scaled to the size of your business.

Do I need all six seats?

No. Many clients start with one seat, often the CFO, and add others as priorities change. The advantage of Claremore is that every seat you add already shares the same view of your business.

How does pricing work?

Each engagement is scoped to your needs. CFO support is also available through our Executive and White-Glove bookkeeping packages. Book a call and we will recommend the right mix.

Will they work with my existing team and vendors?

Yes. Your fractional executives work alongside your staff and current partners, and step in to lead where leadership is missing.
