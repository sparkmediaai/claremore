Why it matters

More software shouldn’t mean more friction.

Most growing companies already have the tools: a website, ad accounts, a CRM, scheduling, email, texting, reporting and a stack of industry software. The problem is that those tools rarely share the right information at the right time. Teams copy data by hand, leads wait for a response, and nobody has a clear picture of what’s working.

Our fractional executives untangle the stack, connect the steps that matter, and measure everything against the numbers your CFO already watches.

Fractional CTO

A technology leader who makes your software work together, and finds where AI is genuinely useful.

  • AI agents & automation: voice agents, missed-call response, SMS and email workflows, scheduling
  • AI consulting & integration: process mapping, AI opportunity assessments, operational dashboards
  • CRM & sales enablement: pipeline design, lead routing, nurturing and database reactivation
  • Integrations between the tools you already own, so staff stop being the integration
