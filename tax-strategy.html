Questions

Frequently asked

Who is the Tax Strategy review for?

Business owners who want a professional look at both their business and personal tax picture, especially if you suspect you’re overpaying, your structure hasn’t been reviewed in a while, or your income has grown.

When does the 21-day clock start?

Once we’ve received all the required documents on your checklist and confirmed them in the verification meeting. We deliver your final presentation within 21 days of that point.

How does the $20,000 guarantee work?

If we don’t identify at least $20,000 in potential tax savings, we refund your full $4,000 fee. Your engagement agreement defines the savings measurement period and review scope.

Does this include filing my tax returns?

No. Tax return preparation and ongoing services are separately scoped and priced. In your final presentation, we’ll review the options if you’d like Claremore to implement your plan.
