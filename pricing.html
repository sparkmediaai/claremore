Questions

Pricing FAQ

Why do prices say “starting at”?

Monthly pricing is based on your expected activity and final scope, such as transaction volume and the number of accounts. We confirm your exact price after a consultation, before you commit.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes. One-time onboarding starts at {{ site.data.packages.onboarding }}. It covers getting your accounts connected, your books set up correctly and your reporting in place.

Can I change packages later?

Yes. Many clients start with the books and reporting they need today and add guidance, payroll or cash-flow management as the business grows.

Is tax preparation included?

Every package includes a year-end tax-ready financial package. Tax return filing and our Tax Strategy review are separate services, and the White-Glove package includes quarterly tax-planning check-ins.
