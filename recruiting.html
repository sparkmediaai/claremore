The problem

Hiring is a second job you never signed up for.

Writing the posting, sorting through applications, chasing references and scheduling interviews can swallow weeks of an owner’s time. And a rushed hire is expensive to undo.

  • Open roles sit unfilled while the team covers the gap
  • Hundreds of applications, very few real fits
  • Interviews squeezed between everything else
  • No clear sense of what the role should pay
